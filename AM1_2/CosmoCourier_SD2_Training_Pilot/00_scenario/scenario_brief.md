# Cosmo Courier Co. — Scenario Brief

## SD2 Training Pilot: Style and Structure Pilot

---

## The Organisation

**Cosmo Courier Co.** is a fictional intergalactic parcel delivery service operating six regular routes between space station hubs. Founded in 2019, the company now handles thousands of deliveries each month across four delivery sectors.

The three members of the operations team you will be working alongside are:

- **Zara Voss** — Operations Manager. Zara wants clear, simple answers about where the business is performing well and where it needs attention.
- **Blix Tanaka** — Data Coordinator. Blix has cleaned and treated the delivery data and is handing it over to you for analysis.
- **Korvus Neel** — Sector Performance Lead. Korvus is particularly interested in whether one region is consistently outperforming the others.

---

## The Business Question

> **"Which delivery regions and service types are generating the most revenue, and is there a relationship between parcel weight and delivery fee?"**

Zara needs an answer before the next operations meeting. She wants to know:
- Which region brings in the most money.
- Which service type drives the most revenue.
- Whether heavier parcels predict higher fees.
- Whether any part of the business needs attention.

---

## Your Role

You have been given a **cleaned and treated dataset** from Blix.

The data is ready for analysis. Your job is to:

1. Explore the dataset using formulas and filters.
2. Summarise patterns using Pivot Tables and charts.
3. Build a simple dashboard.
4. Interpret what the data shows and report back to Zara.

---

## The Dataset

**File:** `01_source_data/sd2_start.xlsx`

The dataset covers **72 delivery records** from January to June 2025.

It includes:
- Delivery reference codes
- Delivery dates
- Region (four delivery sectors)
- Service type (Standard, Express, Priority)
- Parcel weight in kilograms
- Delivery fee in pounds
- Delivery time in minutes
- Customer satisfaction rating (1–5)

The data is clean and ready to analyse. You do not need to import, merge or clean anything. You simply open it and begin.

---

## What You Need to Know Before You Start

You have been given **clean data**. That means:
- All column names are clear.
- All data types are correct.
- There are no duplicates.
- There are no blank key fields.
- The data is sorted by date.

However, the meaning of the data is not obvious yet. Your job is to find the patterns.

---

## The Three Workbooks

This scenario has three workbooks, each building on the last.

| Workbook | Focus | Difficulty |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Workbook 1 | Formulas, Filters and Flags | 3/10 |
| Workbook 2 | Pivots, Charts, Slicers and Timelines | 5/10 |
| Workbook 3 | Dashboard, Trendline and Interpretation | 7/10 |

Start with Workbook 1 and work through in order.
