# DRAGONFLIGHT DELIVERIES LTD
## Scenario Demonstration 2 — Training Pilot
## Scenario Brief

---

### The Organisation

**Dragonflight Deliveries Ltd** is the Realm's most reliable parcel courier service.

Instead of vans, they use dragons.

Instead of a sorting depot, they have a Dragon Dispatch Yard.

Instead of tracking numbers, deliveries are logged by dragon name, route and cargo type.

Business is booming. Deliveries are up. Tips are rolling in.

But nobody has actually stopped to look at what the data is telling them.

That is where you come in.

---

### The People You Will Meet

**Ember Scale** — Chief Dragon Officer

Ember oversees all flight operations. She wants to know which routes are performing well and which are dragging down revenue. She is suspicious that Mountain Pass is hiding something impressive. She is also suspicious that Coastal Hop is hiding something embarrassing.

**Pebble Grimstone** — Logistics Wizard

Pebble manages cargo allocation and scheduling. He believes heavier packages take longer to fly. He would like to see the data confirm this — or prove him wrong. He is genuinely unsure which outcome he prefers.

**Ash Wingmore** — Dispatch Coordinator

Ash handles customer ratings and on-time records. She has noticed that some deliveries are consistently late and some customers consistently unhappy. She wants a clear flag system so she can identify which deliveries need a closer look.

---

### The Business Question

> **Which delivery routes and cargo categories generate the most revenue for Dragonflight Deliveries Ltd, and is there a relationship between cargo weight and flight time?**

---

### What You Have Been Given

You have been given a **cleaned and treated dataset** containing 75 delivery records from January to September 2025.

The dataset is ready for analysis.

It does not require importing, merging, appending or cleaning.

Your job is to analyse the dataset and explain what it shows.

---

### What the Data Covers

Each row in the dataset represents one completed delivery.

The dataset records:

- When the delivery happened
- Which dragon flew the route
- Which route was used
- What type of cargo was carried
- How heavy the cargo was
- How long the flight took
- How much revenue was generated
- Whether a tip was received
- How the customer rated the delivery
- Whether the delivery was on time

---

### What Needs to Be Done

The data is ready to analyse, but the meaning is not obvious yet.

Ember, Pebble and Ash need someone to:

1. Use formulas to summarise the data and flag records for review
2. Use Pivot Tables, charts, slicers and timelines to find patterns
3. Build a clear dashboard that tells the story at a glance
4. Use a scatter chart to explore the weight-versus-time question
5. Write a plain-English explanation of what the data shows, what it suggests, and what it does not prove

### Tone

Ember, Pebble and Ash are friendly colleagues, not an assessment panel.

There are no wrong attempts here, only useful discoveries.

If the data surprises you, that is good. Surprises are often where the insights are.
