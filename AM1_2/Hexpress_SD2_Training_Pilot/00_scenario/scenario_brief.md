# Hexpress SD2 Training Pilot — Scenario Brief

---

## Organisation

**Hexpress Delivery Co.**

The fastest dragon-courier network in the known realm.

Hexpress operates a fleet of four trained delivery dragons, each covering routes across five delivery zones: North, South, East, West, and Central.
The company handles three types of packages: Standard, Express, and Fragile.

Customers rate every delivery on a score from 1 to 10.
Revenue is generated per delivery, based on distance and package type.

---

## The Staff

**Ember Ashwick** — Senior Dragon Handler

Ember manages the day-to-day welfare of the dragon fleet.
She is deeply fond of Scorchwing (do not tell the others).
She wants to know which dragons are working hardest and which might need extra training.

---

**Cinders Bramblewood** — Data Analyst

Cinders keeps the delivery logs and has prepared the cleaned and treated dataset for this analysis.
She is meticulous, slightly competitive, and has strong opinions about conditional formatting.

---

**Blaze Ironforge** — Operations Manager

Blaze needs clear answers, quickly.
He does not read long reports.
He wants a dashboard that tells the story in under thirty seconds.

---

## The Business Question

**Which dragons and delivery zones are performing best, and is there a relationship between delivery distance and delivery time?**

Hexpress needs to answer this question using the delivery data from January to June 2026.

---

## What You Have Been Given

You have been given a cleaned and treated dataset.

The file is:

    01_source_data/sd2_start.xlsx

The dataset covers 75 deliveries made between January and June 2026.

It includes information about:

- which dragon made each delivery
- which zone the delivery went to
- what type of package was sent
- how far the delivery travelled
- how long the delivery took
- how much the customer rated the delivery
- how much revenue the delivery generated

The data is ready to analyse.
The data preparation is already complete.
Your job is to analyse the dataset and explain what it shows.

---

## What You Need to Do

1. Check the dataset is suitable for analysis.
2. Use formulas to calculate summary statistics.
3. Use conditional formulas to answer specific questions.
4. Create an IF review flag for unusual deliveries.
5. Use filters to focus on particular groups.
6. Use conditional formatting to highlight unusual values.
7. Build Pivot Tables and charts to summarise the data.
8. Add slicers and a timeline to allow interactive exploration.
9. Build a clean dashboard.
10. Create a scatter chart and add a trendline.
11. Write a clear, plain-English interpretation of what the data shows.
12. State what the data suggests, what it does not prove, and any limitations.

---

## Why This Matters

Blaze is presenting to the Hexpress board next month.
He needs to show which dragons are delivering the best results, which zones are most profitable, and whether there is evidence that longer routes take proportionally longer to fly.

A clear, well-structured dashboard and a confident interpretation will make all the difference.

Cinders has done the hard work of preparing the data.
Your job is to make sense of it.

---

## Tone and Approach

This is a training exercise, not a high-stakes assessment.

Work through the workbooks at a comfortable pace.
Take time to notice patterns.
Check your results as you go.
Ask yourself: "Does this make sense?"

You are building skills that will make you a more confident and capable data technician.
