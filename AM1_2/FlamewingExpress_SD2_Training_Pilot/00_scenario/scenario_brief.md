# Flamewing Express — SD2 Scenario Brief

---

## Organisation

**Flamewing Express** is a dragon-powered parcel delivery company serving five magical realms across the known world. Founded by the legendary courier Agatha Burnsworth, the company has grown from a single fire-breather to a fleet of five experienced dragon riders — each with their own territory, style and reputation.

Headquarters: The Smouldering Depot, Ironforge Pass
Motto: *"If it does not arrive on fire, we did not try hard enough."*

## The Business Question

Baron Smokington has called a quarterly review. He needs to know:

> **"Which riders and routes are generating the most revenue? Is there a relationship between delivery distance and income? And is Crystal Coast as underperforming as Iggy Cinders keeps insisting?"**

---

## The Dataset

You have been given a **cleaned and treated dataset** covering 75 deliveries made by Flamewing Express during 2025.

The data is ready to analyse. Your job is to find the patterns, visualise the results, and explain what the data shows.

The data preparation has already been completed. You do not need to import, clean or merge any files.

**Source file:** `01_source_data/sd2_start.xlsx`

---

## Why the Analysis Matters

Iggy Cinders has a hunch. Petra Scales has a suspicion. Baron Smokington wants numbers.

The cleaned dataset contains patterns — but the meaning is not obvious yet. Your analysis will reveal which riders are pulling their weight, which routes are underperforming, whether distance predicts revenue, and whether the summer peak that Petra noticed is real or imagined.
