# Scenario Brief — Orbit Express Delivery Ltd

---

## Your Organisation

**Orbit Express Delivery Ltd** is the galaxy's most reliable parcel delivery company.

From their headquarters on Starport Alpha, they dispatch couriers across five interstellar routes, delivering everything from standard packages to hazardous materials — all guaranteed to arrive (mostly) on time.

The company runs four couriers:

- **Zara Comet** — fast, reliable, very popular with customers
- **Max Quasar** — experienced, handles difficult routes
- **Bip-9000** — the team robot, methodical and highly rated
- **Lyra Stardust** — newest member, still building her reputation

The operations team is led by three recurring staff members you will meet throughout this pack:

| Name | Role | Catchphrase |
|------|------|-------------|
| Director Valentina Solaris | Runs the whole operation | "The data will tell us everything. Probably." |
| Route Manager Cosmo Wrenchy | Handles logistics and route planning | "If the parcel arrives, it counts." |
| Data Analyst Unit BLEEP-7 | Robot analyst, always thorough | "Calculating optimal curiosity levels now." |

---

## The Business Question

> **Which delivery routes and parcel types are generating the most revenue — and is there a relationship between parcel weight and delivery cost?**

Valentina has been looking at the numbers and suspects that some routes are carrying the business while others are barely worth running. She also wonders whether heavier parcels reliably cost more to send.

Cosmo thinks the Nebula Dash route is the star performer. BLEEP-7 wants evidence.

Your job is to find out.

---

## What You Have Been Given

You have been given a **cleaned and treated dataset**.

The data is ready to analyse. No imports, no merging, no fixing required.

The dataset covers parcel deliveries made between **January 2024 and September 2024**.

It records:

- which route each parcel travelled
- what type of parcel it was
- which courier handled it
- the parcel weight
- the delivery distance
- the revenue earned
- the delivery time
- the customer rating
- whether the delivery was on time

---

## Your Mission

The data is clean. The meaning is not obvious yet.

Your job is to:

1. Explore the dataset using summary statistics and conditional formulas.
2. Spot patterns using filters and conditional formatting.
3. Summarise the data using Pivot Tables and charts.
4. Build a clear dashboard that tells the story.
5. Use a scatter chart and trendline to investigate the weight-versus-revenue relationship.
6. Write a plain-English explanation of what the data shows — and what it does not prove.

---

## Scenario Tone

This is a fun scenario. The parcels are interstellar. The couriers have silly names. The credits are fictional.

But the Excel skills are real.

Every formula, every Pivot Table, every chart you build in this pack is exactly the kind of work a real data technician does with real business data.

BLEEP-7 is watching. In a supportive way.
