# SCENARIO BRIEF — STARBASE MERIDIAN LOGISTICS AUTHORITY

---

## Who we are

Welcome to **Starbase Meridian Logistics Authority (SMLA)**.

We operate from Meridian-9, a busy orbital station responsible for coordinating cargo deliveries between four space sectors: Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Delta.

Every week, cargo pods leave the station carrying supplies, equipment and specialist materials to clients across the sectors. Each delivery is tracked: the sector it went to, the type of cargo service used, the cargo weight, the time it took, and the revenue earned.

---

## The team you are working with

**Commander Petra Volkov (Staff ID: PV01)**
Operations Chief. Petra keeps things running. She cares deeply about which sectors are performing well and which might need attention. She is great at spotting patterns but does not always have time to crunch numbers herself.

**Tech Analyst Remi Okafor (Staff ID: RO02)**
Data Coordinator. Remi is meticulous. He has spent the last month collecting and cleaning the delivery records. He has handed the cleaned dataset over to you and wants to know what it shows.

**Dispatcher Zara Quintero (Staff ID: ZQ03)**
Cargo Scheduler. Zara manages the Economy delivery queue. She suspects something is off with how those deliveries are performing — but she cannot point to the numbers. She is hoping you can.

---

## The situation

Remi has cleaned and treated the delivery records for the year 2025.

You have been given a clean, ready-to-analyse dataset.

**Your job is to analyse the dataset and explain what it shows.**

The data is ready. The meaning is not obvious yet. That is where you come in.

---

## The business question

> **"Which cargo sectors and delivery types are generating the most revenue — and is there a relationship between cargo weight and revenue earned?"**

Commander Volkov has asked for a clear answer she can use when planning next year's delivery schedule.

---

## What you need to produce

By the end of the three workbooks, you will have:

- A set of summary statistics and conditional formula results
- A Pivot Table and Pivot Chart showing performance by sector
- A dashboard with summary cards, a chart, a slicer and a timeline
- A scatter chart showing the relationship between cargo weight and revenue
- A written interpretation explaining what the data shows — and what it does not prove

---

## The dataset

**File:** `01_source_data/sd2_start.xlsx`

The dataset contains **75 cargo delivery records** from 2025.

It is clean. It has clear headers. It is ready for analysis.

You do not need to clean it, merge it, or import anything.
