# SCENARIO BRIEF

## Drakonex Dragon Express
*A friendly, fictional medieval parcel-delivery company.*

---

## The Setting

Drakonex Dragon Express is a parcel delivery company in a fictional medieval kingdom. Instead of vans, they use trained dragons. Instead of warehouses, they use mountain roosts. Instead of barcodes, they use enchanted wax seals.

It is a busy place. Dragons fly. Parcels travel. Spreadsheets multiply.

The company runs weekly delivery sprints. Each sprint covers a region of villages, towns and outposts. After each sprint, the data team needs to check what happened.

That is where you come in.

---

## The Recurring Cast

You will see these three people throughout the workbooks. They are friendly. They are on your side.

**Captain Aelwyn Pyrfeld** — *Flight Operations Chief*
She runs the dragon roster and the weekly sprints. She is calm, organised, and asks short questions. She wants to know whether the sprint went smoothly.

**Bramble Quickstitch** — *Parcel Handling Lead*
He looks after parcels: how many, what weight, where they went. He is cheerful and curious. He likes a good puzzle.

**Marigold Tinderwhisk** — *Senior Data Clerk*
She is your guide. She has cleaned thousands of messy spreadsheets and is happy to share what she has learned. She will pop up throughout the workbooks with tips, encouragement and the occasional gentle warning.

---

## The Business Question

> *Did last weekend's delivery sprint reach the right villages with the right number of parcels, using the right dragons?*

Captain Aelwyn needs a clean, joined-up view of the sprint so she can answer this in Monday's planning meeting. She does not need a finished report yet. She needs the underlying data to be tidy, complete and trustworthy.

---

## Why The Data Needs Cleaning

Three different people record three different parts of the sprint:

- The **flight clerks** log each delivery as it happens. Their notes go into a spreadsheet. They are quick typists, but tired by the end of the day. Some rows are duplicated. Some have blanks. Some have wobbly capital letters.
- The **roost-keepers** keep the dragon register in a separate file. Most dragon IDs match the delivery log, but a few do not.
- The **route map** is a printed reference table that shows what each route code means. It lives in a PDF.

To answer Captain Aelwyn's question, you need to bring all three files together, clean them up, and check the result.

---

## Your Mission Across The Three Workbooks

**Workbook 1** — *Import, Inspect and Spot Patterns*
Get the three files safely into Excel. Take a first look around. Notice what feels off without trying to fix everything.

**Workbook 2** — *Clean, Fix and Prepare Keys*
Tidy up the data. Trim spaces. Fix capital letters. Decide what to do with blanks and duplicates. Get the key fields ready for joining.

**Workbook 3** — *Merge, Append and Validate*
Bring the cleaned tables together. Add columns from one to another. Stack rows where it makes sense. Then check the result.
