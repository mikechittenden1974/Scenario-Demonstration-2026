# Welcome to Nimbus Nine

The friendliest space station this side of the asteroid belt.

## The Place

Nimbus Nine is a small but very busy cargo station orbiting a gentle blue moon.
Cargo couriers fly in and out all day, delivering parcels to colonies, asteroid
miners, and one very picky robot tea shop on Sector 4.

The station is famous for two things:
1. The best space waffles in the galaxy.
2. A slightly chaotic delivery log.

## The Crew

You will hear from these three crew members throughout the workbooks.

**Captain Vega Sparrowsong** — Station Commander.
Cheerful, calm, and always says "we have got this" before taking a sip of tea.

**Engineer Pip Quasar** — Cargo and Data Officer.
Loves spreadsheets a worrying amount. Will high-five you for cleaning a column.

**Quartermaster Thessaly Comet** — Logistics Lead.
Carries a clipboard everywhere. Has a soft spot for fragile cargo.

## The Business Question

> Which cargo routes are running smoothly, and which routes need a little help?

Captain Vega wants a clear, clean view of the last batch of deliveries so the
team can spot patterns and plan next month's rotas.

## Why The Data Needs Work

Right now, the answer lives across three separate files.

* A deliveries log (the main activity table).
* A couriers roster (who flies what).
* A routes reference (which routes go where).

The files are not joined up. There are duplicates, blanks, odd spelling, and
mismatched codes. Before anyone can answer the Captain's question, the data
needs to be **imported, cleaned, joined, and checked**.

That is your job in this SD1 Training Pilot.

## What You Will Do

You will work through three short workbooks.

* Workbook 1 — Import, inspect, and spot patterns.
* Workbook 2 — Clean, fix, and prepare keys.
* Workbook 3 — Merge, append, and validate.

By the end you will have one neat, trustworthy file that answers the
Captain's question.

## A Friendly Reminder

This is a confidence-building pilot.

Mistakes are normal. Undo is your friend. There are no surprises and no
gotchas. Just take your time, click slowly, and read each step.

You have got this.
