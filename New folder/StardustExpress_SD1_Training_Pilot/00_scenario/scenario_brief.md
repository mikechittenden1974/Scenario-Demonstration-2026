# Scenario Brief: Stardust Express

## The Organisation

Welcome to **Stardust Express**, a small but cheerful intergalactic parcel delivery service operating out of a converted asteroid called Hopper Station. We deliver everything from glowing space-tulips to spare engine bolts across twelve known sectors of the local star cluster.

We are a friendly crew. The work is fast-paced. The data, sadly, is not always tidy.

## The Crew You Will Meet

You will work alongside three recurring members of the Stardust Express team throughout this training pilot.

**Captain Nova Hartwell** is the Logistics Coordinator. She sets the missions and explains why the data work matters. She is calm, practical, and famously fond of strong tea.

**Pip Quasar** is your fellow Apprentice Data Technician. Pip has been on the job a few weeks longer than you and will share friendly tips. Pip once accidentally appended the canteen menu to a delivery report and lived to tell the tale.

**Glim Bracket** is the station Quartermaster. He needs clean delivery data so he can plan crew rotas, fuel orders and sector resupplies. Glim is patient but he does sigh quietly when the numbers do not add up.

## The Business Question

> Which delivery routes are busiest this quarter, and are there any deliveries that look unusual or unmatched, so that Glim can plan the next resupply with confidence?

To answer this, the team needs the delivery records imported, cleaned, joined to the courier and route information, appended with the late-arriving extra deliveries, and then checked.

## Why This Data Work Matters

Stardust Express has four small data files sitting on the station server. They came from different systems and they do not yet talk to each other.

The main delivery records live in one file. A second file contains a handful of late-arriving deliveries that need to be added in. A third file lists the couriers. A fourth file is a printed lookup of route names and sectors.

Captain Nova has asked you to bring these files together using Excel, so the team can finally see the full picture for the quarter.

You will use **Power Query** as your main tool. Power Query is Excel's tool for importing, cleaning and reshaping data.

## What You Will Build

Across three calm, friendly workbooks you will:

1. Import the four source files and have a first look.
2. Clean and shape the data with Power Query.
3. Append the late deliveries, merge in courier and route details, and validate the result.

By the end you will have produced a single tidy table called sd1_complete.xlsx that Glim can use to plan the next resupply.

## Tone for the Pilot

This pilot is meant to feel:

- Calm
- Curious
- A little bit silly in places
- Confidence-building

Mistakes are expected. Undo is your friend. Tea is encouraged.

Welcome aboard the Hopper Station. Let us get started.
