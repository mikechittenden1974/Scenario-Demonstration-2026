# Wyrmwing Express Dragon Delivery Co.

## SD1 Training Pilot — Scenario Brief

Welcome to Wyrmwing Express, the friendliest dragon-powered parcel service in the
six skies. We move enchanted packages, perishable potions, and the occasional
grumpy goblin between mountain stations, coastal eyries and the floating market
at Cloudfen.

This pilot uses a small, friendly dataset so you can practise the core data
technician moves without any pressure.

---

## The team you will work with

You will see three recurring colleagues across the workbooks. They are friendly,
patient, and they make plenty of mistakes themselves.

**Captain Esme Brightscale** — Operations Lead. She knows every route and every
dragon by name. Esme cares about the data because she rosters the flight teams.

**Postmaster Theo Marrowfen** — Data Steward. Theo keeps the records and is
always asking, "How do we know that number is right?" Theo will be your guide
when something looks odd.

**Junior Rider Pip Hollowoak** — Trainee. Pip joined Wyrmwing last month and is
learning alongside you. Pip is allowed to ask "silly" questions, and so are you.

---

## The business question

> Which delivery routes are bringing in the most parcels and the most coin,
> and are any of our dragons being overworked?

Esme wants to look at last month's numbers and decide which routes to grow,
which routes to retire, and which dragons need a longer rest between flights.

---

## Why we need to import, clean, append, merge and check

The Wyrmwing data lives in a few small files that were built by different
people in different sky-stations. None of it is broken. It is just messy in
the friendly way that real data tends to be.

To answer Esme's question, you will need to:

- **import** four small source files into Excel
- **look** at what is in each file before changing anything
- **clean** the activity records so the columns and labels are consistent
- **append** an extra week of activity onto the main activity table
- **merge** the activity table with the dragon roster and the route lookup
- **validate** the result so we can trust the numbers we hand to Esme

That is it. Nothing scary. We will move one small step at a time.

---

## How this pilot works

This SD1 Training Pilot is split into three short workbooks.

1. **Workbook 1 — Import and First Look.** A gentle introduction to bringing
   files into Excel and noticing what is there.
2. **Workbook 2 — Power Query Cleaning Toolkit.** A friendly tour of the
   Power Query cleaning tools, in the order a real data technician uses them.
3. **Workbook 3 — Append, Merge and Validate.** Putting the pieces together
   and checking the result.

You will land at a single clean table called `sd1_complete.xlsx` that Postmaster
Theo can hand to Captain Esme with a quiet, confident nod.
