# Scenario Brief — Apex Sports Academy

## Organisation
**Apex Sports Academy** is a mid-sized multi-sport facility based in Sheffield, offering group fitness sessions, personal coaching, and facility hire to registered members.

## Staff
- **Jamie** — Data Analyst (your role in this scenario)
- **Priya** — Operations Manager
- **Marcus** — Head Coach

## Scenario Title
Session Booking and Member Engagement Analysis 2024

## Business Question
Which session types, facilities, and membership tiers generate the highest bookings and attendance rates, and what patterns suggest members are at risk of disengaging?

## Dataset Story
Apex Sports Academy records every session booking made by its members across five facilities. The dataset covers January to December 2024 and uses the booking as the unit of analysis. Each booking links to a member record and a facility/session lookup. Priya needs this analysis to decide which sessions to expand, which to retire, and which members to target with retention campaigns. Marcus wants to know if coaching style or session type affects attendance. Jamie has been asked to prepare the data and produce an analysis report.

## Time Period
January 2024 — December 2024

## Unit of Analysis
Individual session booking
