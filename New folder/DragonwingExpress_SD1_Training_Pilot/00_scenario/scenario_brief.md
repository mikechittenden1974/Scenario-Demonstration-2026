# Dragonwing Express — Scenario Brief

## The Organisation

Welcome to **Dragonwing Express**, the friendliest dragon-powered parcel delivery service in the four kingdoms. We carry letters, baked goods, scrolls, and the occasional grumpy cat between cloud-top villages, mountain monasteries, river ports, and one very damp swamp town.

Our motto is simple:

> *"If it fits in a saddlebag, we will fly it there."*

## Meet the Team

You will hear from three friendly Dragonwing colleagues throughout this training pilot.

**Mira Featherstone — Senior Dispatch Coordinator**
Mira keeps the routes, the dragons, and the riders running on time. She is patient, encouraging, and she will be your guide.

**Jasper Inkwell — Records Keeper**
Jasper is the office data fan. He genuinely loves a tidy spreadsheet. He will pop in with helpful tips when something looks unusual.

**Captain Borin Ironscale — Flight Operations Manager**
A retired dragonrider with a big laugh and a bigger moustache. He cares about safe deliveries and happy dragons, in that order.

## The Business Question

Mira wants to answer one simple question for the next quarterly planning meeting:

> *"Which delivery routes were busiest last quarter, and which deliveries had problems we should follow up on?"*

## Why The Data Work Matters

The trouble is, the records have been scattered across three different files for years.

- The **delivery log** is kept in an Excel workbook.
- The **dragon roster** is exported from the stable tablet as a CSV.
- The **location lookup table** lives inside a printable PDF that the riders carry with them.

Mira needs all three pulled together into one clean table. That way she can answer her question without flipping between three files at once.

That is your mission across the next three workbooks.

## Your Mission Across SD1

You will work through three short, friendly workbooks.

1. **Workbook 1 — Import, Inspect and Spot Patterns.** You will bring all three files into Excel using Power Query and have a first look around.
2. **Workbook 2 — Clean, Fix and Prepare Keys.** You will tidy the data and get it ready to be combined.
3. **Workbook 3 — Merge, Append and Validate.** You will join the three sources together and check the result is sensible.

By the end you will have one clean, combined table that Mira can show in her meeting.

## Tone Reminder

This training is meant to be fun. Mistakes are completely normal. If something looks odd, that is a clue. Take your time. Use Undo. Try again. You are doing fine.
